How it works

From strangers to your people in three steps

Social media got good at keeping us scrolling. iPerspective is built to get you into a real conversation with people who share how you see things.

1

Join your communities

Your age group, city, work, interests, faith. Answer a few quick questions inside each one so the app knows who you relate to.

2

Get placed in a room

About ten people matched on your actual answers. You can see the reason you were matched, so nothing is hidden behind an algorithm.

3

Play, compare, keep your people

Quick games reveal who everyone really is. Keep the people you click with. Their connections stay when the room resets.

Try it right here

Answer. Guess. Find out.

In the app, every question has a twist: before you see the results, you guess how the other group answered. Guessing how people unlike you think is the whole idea behind iPerspective.

  • Every answer counts toward who you get introduced to
  • See how your communities split, side by side
  • Open a thread with the people who answered like you
